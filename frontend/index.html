Finding us

Visit the clinic

Three minutes from Ratnapura town centre, with parking at the door and the bus stand across the road.

Open Mon–Sat · 8.00am – 6.00pm
1
By bus Any Ratnapura town service stops at the Galle Road halt, directly opposite the clinic. It is a 20-metre walk to the entrance.
2
By car or three-wheeler Turn off the main street at the clock tower and continue 400 m along Galle Road; the clinic is the white building with the green cross on your left.
3
Parking & access Six parking bays in front of the building and a covered drop-off at the door. The entrance and the washrooms are step-free.

Frequently asked questions
