the running application.

## 10. Booking round-trip, after the fix (latest check)

A patient reported that pressing **Confirm the appointment** as `nimasha` answered *"Please choose a
patient, or enter the details of a new one"* - a question that only makes sense at the clinic counter,
where the receptionist chooses between the register and a walk-in.

**What was wrong.** The booking desk is shared by the staff and by a patient. For a patient the
"Who is the appointment for?" field is (correctly) hidden - but the page then sent no patient at all,
and the clinic's rule refused a request that named nobody.

**What changed (one rule, one place each).**

* the page: a signed-in patient always sends their own record; the field is replaced by a small
  "This visit is booked in your own name." line with the patient's own photograph;
* the rule itself (`AppointmentService.resolvePatient`): for a patient the clinic decides the record
  from the sign-in - the page cannot talk it out of that - and a request that names somebody else is
  refused with *"You can only book appointments for yourself"*.

## 12. This round of fixes, and how each one was checked

Five things were asked for after the site was already working. Each was fixed at its cause, then
proved in a browser engine and (where it belongs to the clinic's rules) at the server.

### 12.1 The header button that lost its word on hover

*Cause:* the filled button changed its colour with `filter: brightness(1.07)`. On a gradient pill a
filter makes the browser repaint the element — and on some machines the label disappeared.

*Fix:* the hover state is now a slightly deeper gradient with its own shadow
(`.btn-primary:hover`), the light that sweeps across a button sits **behind** the words
(`.btn > *:not(.ripple) { z-index: 1 }`), and the sweep moves with `transform` instead of `left`, so
no layout work happens on every frame. No `filter: brightness` is left anywhere in the stylesheet.

### 12.2 The clinic documents belong to the system administrator

Three layers, so no page, no address and no request can reach them:

| Layer | What changed |
|---|---|
| Menu | `{ id: 'reports', … roles: ['ADMIN'] }` — the front desk and doctors no longer see the entry |
| Router | `VIEW_ROLES` is built from the menu; a typed address or an old link is refused with a plain message and lands back on the dashboard |
| Server | `ReportController` is annotated `@RequireRole({UserRole.ADMIN})` |

Checked live: `/api/reports` answers **200** for the administrator and **403** for the front desk, a
doctor and a patient; `/api/reports/patients.txt/content` answers **403** for the front desk, so a
single file cannot be read either.

### 12.3 Search that looks like the rest of the site

The old search was an `<input>` with the character `⌕` in front of it. There is now one search box
for the whole portal (`frontend/js/modules/search.js`): a rounded field with a real magnifier icon, a
clear cross that appears only when there is something to clear, a quiet `/` hint, focus ring, and
Escape to empty it. It is used by **patient records, the medical team, appointments, feedback, the
staff list and the activity log**. The audit asserts the old character is gone.

### 12.4 The booking desk's cheap drop-downs

Choosing a doctor and choosing a patient were plain `<select>` boxes; with a real clinic's register
that is a wall of names. Both are now **searchable pickers** (`enhanceSelect`) showing the person's
photograph, their speciality and room, or their telephone number — with arrow keys, Enter, Escape,
type-to-filter and a "Nothing matches that search." line. The page's own logic is untouched: the
picker sets the same `<select>` and fires its usual event, so free times still load the moment a
doctor is chosen.

### 12.5 Steady movement, and more of the site usable from the keyboard

* One calm smooth-scroll loop, only for mouse wheels, that stops itself while the tab is hidden.
* Scroll work is limited to **one paint per frame** (both pages).
* The sticky table head and the compact site header no longer blur — a blur on something that moves
  repaints on every frame. Overlays (windows, drawers) keep theirs.
* `will-change` on the things that move, `contain: layout style` on the long lists, and everything
  pauses while the tab is in the background (`html.tab-hidden`).
* **Focus trap** (`focus-trap`) on every window, so Tab walks the window's own buttons and the focus
  returns to where it was. Windows now announce themselves (`role="dialog" aria-modal="true"`).
* **"g" then a letter** jumps to a page (dashboard, book, appointments, records, team, waiting room,
  schedule, feedback, notifications, documents, account) with a small "g …" hint while it waits;
  **`?`** opens a window listing the shortcuts for that person's role; **`/`** jumps into the search
  box. Typing inside a field never triggers a shortcut.

# 13. The waiting list (second wave of real-world features)

A patient who cannot find a suitable time is not simply turned away: they take a **place on that
doctor's waiting list**, and the next time that is freed is offered to them. The whole path is
delivered — entity, rules, REST, page, text file — and every claim below comes from a run.

## 13.1 Where the work lives

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `appointmentmanagement/WaitlistEntry.java` | the entity: patient, doctor, the dates they can come, a note, the offered time, and the states `WAITING → OFFERED → BOOKED` / `CANCELLED` |
| `appointmentmanagement/WaitlistRepository.java` | the searches: the clinic's board, one doctor's list, one patient's places, and "the people waiting for this doctor, longest first" |
| `appointmentmanagement/WaitlistForm.java` | what the person fills in |
| `appointmentmanagement/WaitlistService.java` | every rule: who may join, who may see what, who may offer or close a place |
| `appointmentmanagement/WaitlistController.java` | `/api/waitlist` — read, join, offer a free time, mark booked, leave |
| `frontend/js/views/waitlist.js` | the page: a table for the clinic, portrait cards for the patient |
| `frontend/js/modules/waitlist.js` | joining the list — the same window is opened from the booking desk |
| `documents/waiting-list.txt` | the text-file evidence (a ninth file of its own) |

## 13.2 The rules, and where they live

* **Abstraction** — the controller only answers HTTP; the service holds the rules; the entry holds
  its own state machine (`offer`, `markBooked`, `cancel`, and `suits(day)`).
* A patient may only see and close **their own** places; the clinic sees the whole list; a doctor
  sees only their own list. Anything else answers **403** with a human sentence.
* A patient cannot take the same place twice.
* **A freed time is only offered when it suits the patient.** `WaitlistEntry.suits(day)` is asked for
  each candidate: the patient waiting longest *whose own dates contain the freed day* is chosen. A
  time nobody can attend is offered to nobody — it simply stays free for anyone to book.
* Booking with a doctor **closes that patient's own place** on that doctor's list.
* If somebody else books the very time that was offered, the offer is **taken back** (`WaitlistEntry.offerTaken()`), the patient keeps their place in the queue and is told why — so no offer is ever left pointing at a time that is already taken.
* Every change writes `waiting-list.txt`, refreshes the clinic summary and lands in the activity log.

## 13.4 A real fault found and fixed while building this

The clinic summary lives at `reports/clinic-summary.txt`. Its name contains a folder, and Spring's
`/{fileName}/content` address cannot carry a folder — so **the Documents page could not open or
download that one file** (404), the sign-in record was unreachable in the same way, and nothing had
noticed because the lockdown checks only asked whether they were *refused*. Every file now also
carries an address-safe **key** (`reports__clinic-summary.txt`); the page links with the key, the
server resolves by key, plain name or constant, and a downloaded file is named without its folder.
`round3.js` now opens and downloads **all eleven files** on the page as the administrator, and the
waiting-list check reads the summary the same way.
